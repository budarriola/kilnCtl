Each channel's "control fault_guard" line, where shown, comes from GET /api/profile_exec's per-zone status, matched to this channel by index -- this codebase's legacy convention of zone N reading channel N (zones_config_apply_cal()'s scope note). It reports the profile executor's own guard-trip reason for that zone's control loop, which is a different signal from the MAX31856 fault-register bits above it: a zone can show a control fault_guard with a perfectly healthy thermocouple (e.g. a cross-zone plausibility trip), and a channel can show OPEN/OVUV faults with no zone control fault at all (e.g. no profile running). They are shown together here because both answer "what's wrong with this channel," not because they are the same measurement.
