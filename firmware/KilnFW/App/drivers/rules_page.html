Relays & Rules

Saved rules are NOT evaluated by the firmware yet -- there is no rule-evaluator task. Nothing here turns a relay on or off automatically until that's built (TODO.md section 6). Manual relay control on the Dashboard is unaffected by anything saved here.

Hand-typed format, one line each. A relay's rules are OR'd together; a rule's conditions are AND'd together.

RELAY <n 1-4> DRIVEN <0|1>
R <rule 0-2> TEMP <zone 0-2> <GE|LE> <threshold_c>
R <rule 0-2> TIME <GE|LE> <seconds>
R <rule 0-2> RELAY <other 1-4> <0|1>

Example: vent relay 2 opens once zone 0 passes 500C
  RELAY 2 DRIVEN 1
  R 0 TEMP 0 GE 500.0

Relay contact wear

Lifetime on/off transitions per relay, counted by the control loop and kept across reboots. These are electromechanical relays: their contacts are good for roughly 100,000 operations, so a number climbing toward six figures means that relay is nearing the end of its life and should be replaced before it welds shut during a firing.
