"Largest free block" (not "free bytes") is the number that matters on this board: internal DRAM is its tight resource, not total heap. This firmware has measured a 9216-byte largest free block against LVGL's 8192-byte task stack allocation -- about 1 KB of margin. If the internal DRAM row above ever drops close to 8 KB, LVGL's stack allocation is the thing at risk, not some other subsystem's heap use. PSRAM's numbers exist for completeness; this board's 8 MB octal PSRAM has an order of magnitude more slack and is not the resource to watch.
