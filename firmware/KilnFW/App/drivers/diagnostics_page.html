"Largest free block" (not "free bytes") is the number that matters on this board: internal DRAM is its tight resource, not total heap. This firmware has measured a 9216-byte largest free block against LVGL's 8192-byte task stack allocation -- about 1 KB of margin. If the internal DRAM row above ever drops close to 8 KB, LVGL's stack allocation is the thing at risk, not some other subsystem's heap use. PSRAM's numbers exist for completeness; this board's 8 MB octal PSRAM has an order of magnitude more slack and is not the resource to watch.

Thermocouple faults

Loading…

Each channel's "control fault_guard" line, where shown, comes from GET /api/profile_exec's per-zone status, matched to this channel by index -- this codebase's legacy convention of zone N reading channel N (zones_config_apply_cal()'s scope note). It reports the profile executor's own guard-trip reason for that zone's control loop, which is a different signal from the MAX31856 fault-register bits above it: a zone can show a control fault_guard with a perfectly healthy thermocouple (e.g. a cross-zone plausibility trip), and a channel can show OPEN/OVUV faults with no zone control fault at all (e.g. no profile running). They are shown together here because both answer "what's wrong with this channel," not because they are the same measurement.

Task-watchdog panic (dev only)

Panic on hung task--

Disabling this stops a hung task from rebooting the board (dev/bench debugging only, e.g. sitting at a breakpoint) -- it does NOT stop the hang itself. A disabled panic is written to flash and survives a reboot; the setting is shown here and in the dashboard status so it is never silently on. Starting a firing while this is disabled requires an extra confirmation.

Ramp assist

Ramp assist--

Ramp assist is for real firings, not PID tuning or A/B controller comparisons. When enabled, the executor may automatically stretch ramps and shorten dwells to keep up with the kiln's real capability -- a tuning or comparison run needs the RAW, unassisted schedule to produce comparable tracking-error numbers. Leave this OFF for tuning. The setting is written to flash and survives a reboot; it is shown here and in the dashboard status so it is never silently on.

Danger Zone

Manual relay/heater/thermocouple bench test. This section turns off this board's own gate on its four heater relays -- you can force any of them on or off directly, for testing a relay, a contactor, or a thermocouple by hand. The safety processor (SaftyFW) is not bypassed: entering this mode requests nothing from it on its own. The "Firing mode" tile below the four relays sends it the same enable request a real firing sends -- shown here as one more toggle even though it is not actually one of the four ESP relays -- and its own independent guards (over-temperature, E-stop, current sense, and the rest) decide whether the safety relay actually closes, exactly as they would during a firing, watching over you the whole time.

This mode auto-exits after 5 minutes with no activity in this section -- heat-enable released and the relay gate restored, same as if the firing it stood in for had ended, no reboot. Every accepted command here resets that 5 minutes.
